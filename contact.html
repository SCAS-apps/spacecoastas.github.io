If you need your membership status right now

  • Signed in at scas.fish, your status and paid-through date are on your membership page.
  • From the mobile number the club has on file, text STATUS to the club number for an automatic reply with your status, renewal date and anything owing, or NUMBER for your member number.
  • Otherwise email us and we will look it up.

If you would like texts instead of email

Use the text message sign-up. A club officer matches each request to a member record before anything is sent, and you can reply STOP at any time.
