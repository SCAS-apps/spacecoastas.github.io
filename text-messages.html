Get club updates by text message

Members of the Space Coast Aquarium Society can ask us to text them membership renewal reminders, event and swap meet updates, and a link when a new newsletter is published.

Sign up for text messages

Thank you — your request is recorded.

A club officer will match your request to your membership record before any message is sent. You can reply STOP to any message to stop them, or email socials@spacecoastas.org.

This only records your request. Nobody is texted until a club officer has matched the number to your membership, and you can stop at any time by replying STOP.

What to expect

What we text

  • A reminder when your membership is due to renew, with the amount and how to pay.
  • Reminders about club events, swap meets and tables you have reserved.
  • A link when a new club newsletter is published.

How often, and what it costs

Message frequency varies, with no more than about 4 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply — your mobile plan's usual charges still apply.

Checking your membership by text

From the mobile number the club has on file, text STATUS for your membership status, renewal date and anything owing, NUMBER for your member number (handy at our auctions), or HELP for help. Replying to those keywords does not sign you up for any other messages.

Stopping or starting again

Reply STOP to any message to stop them immediately — no need to explain or contact anyone. Text START to begin again, and HELP if you need us. Consent is never a condition of membership.

See also our messaging terms, privacy policy and terms of use.
